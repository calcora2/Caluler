About the Body Surface Area Calculator

The Mosteller formula estimates body surface area from height and weight. Different published formulas can return different estimates, so this result is for information and must not be used on its own for medication dosing or clinical decisions.

How to use

  1. Enter height in centimetres and body weight in kilograms.
  2. Calculate body surface area and review the result in square metres (m²).
  3. Use the value as a formula-based estimate only. Do not use this calculator alone to determine medication doses or make clinical decisions.

Formula and worked example

This page uses the Mosteller equation: body surface area (m²) = square root of [height (cm) × weight (kg) ÷ 3600]. At 170 cm and 70 kg, the estimate is √(170 × 70 ÷ 3600) ≈ 1.82 m².

Body surface area is a calculated estimate. Do not use this result by itself to determine medication doses or clinical treatment.

What is the Mosteller formula?
