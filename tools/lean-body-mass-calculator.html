About the Lean Body Mass Calculator

Lean body mass is estimated by subtracting estimated fat mass from total body weight. The result includes muscle, bone, organs and body water; it is not a direct measurement of skeletal muscle.

How to use

  1. Enter body weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter a body-fat percentage from a consistent measurement method, then calculate the result.
  3. Review lean mass and fat mass together. The estimate depends strongly on the accuracy of the body-fat percentage entered.

Formula and worked example

Fat-free mass = body weight × (1 − body-fat percentage ÷ 100); fat mass = body weight × body-fat percentage ÷ 100. At 75 kg and an estimated 15% body fat, fat-free mass is 63.75 kg and fat mass is 11.25 kg.

The output depends directly on the body-fat percentage entered. If that estimate is inaccurate, the calculated lean and fat masses will also be inaccurate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is lean body mass calculated?

Lean body mass equals body weight multiplied by one minus body-fat percentage divided by 100.

Does lean body mass mean muscle mass?

No. It includes muscle, bone, organs, water and other non-fat tissue.

How reliable is the result?

The calculation depends on how accurately body-fat percentage was measured or estimated. Enter weight from 20–350 kg and body fat from 0–70%.
