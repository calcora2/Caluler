About this Loan Amortization Calculator

Calculate loan repayments using durations measured in days, weeks, months or years. Leave the annual interest rate blank for a zero-interest loan, select daily, weekly, monthly or yearly payments, and optionally add extra principal payments. The calculator separates principal and interest for every payment period and estimates total repayment and interest savings.

How to use

  1. Enter the original loan amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate, or leave it blank for a 0% interest loan.
  3. Enter the loan term and select Days, Weeks, Months or Years.
  4. Select how often repayments occur: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly.
  5. Optionally enter additional principal to pay with each scheduled payment.
  6. Select Calculate Schedule. Review the totals and detailed repayment table.
  7. Use Copy Summary or Download CSV to save the results.

Calculation method

For each payment period, interest is estimated as the opening balance multiplied by the annual interest rate and the fraction of a 365.2425-day year covered by that period. A blank interest rate is treated as zero. The regular payment is calculated to repay the balance over the selected term using those period rates.

The calculator uses average durations of 7 days per week, 365.2425 days per year and 365.2425 divided by 12 days per month. If the selected loan term ends between scheduled payment dates, the final repayment period may be shorter than a full interval.

Worked example

A 10,000-unit loan at 12% annual interest over 12 months, with monthly payments and no extra principal, has an estimated scheduled payment of about 888.49 units and total interest of about 661.85 units. With a blank interest rate, the same principal is repaid without interest, subject to the selected term and payment frequency.

How extra payments work

An extra principal payment is added to each regular payment. This generally reduces the outstanding principal faster and can reduce total interest and the number of payments. The extra amount is per selected payment interval, not necessarily per month.

Important assumptions

This calculator provides an estimate using a fixed annual rate and a proportional day-count method. Actual lenders may use different day-count conventions, compounding schedules, payment dates, fees, penalties or rounding rules. Verify the actual payoff amount with your lender before making financial decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate a loan without interest?

Yes. Leave the annual interest rate blank or enter 0. The calculator will calculate the repayment schedule without interest charges.

Can I use days, weeks, months or years?

Yes. Select the loan term unit separately from the payment frequency. For example, a term can be entered in months with weekly repayments, or in days with daily repayments.

What does extra principal per payment mean?

It is the additional amount applied to the outstanding principal each time a regular payment is made. It can shorten the payoff period and lower interest in this estimate.

Why might the result differ from my lender?

Lenders may use specific calendar dates, daily compounding, fees, insurance, different rounding rules or other contract terms that this estimate does not include.

Are my loan details saved or sent anywhere?

The calculation is designed to run in your browser. Avoid entering private or sensitive information into any online tool unless you have reviewed the site's privacy information.
