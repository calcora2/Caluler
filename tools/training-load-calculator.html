Session RPE training-load method

Multiply workout duration by your perceived exertion rating. Use the same rating scale and reporting timing to compare sessions.

Session RPE Training Load Formula

Session training load (arbitrary units) = workout duration in minutes × the session rating of perceived exertion (RPE).

Worked example

A 45-minute session rated 5 out of 10 gives a training load of 45 × 5 = 225 arbitrary units.

How to rate effort

Use the same 0–10 scale and record the rating consistently after each session. RPE is subjective, so the score is most useful for comparing your own sessions using the same method.

Limitations

This score is a simple workload tracking measure; it does not diagnose fatigue or injury and should not be used alone to prescribe training. Consider recovery, symptoms and professional coaching guidance as appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is session RPE?

Session RPE combines how hard a workout felt with its duration to estimate internal training load.

What is the formula?

Multiply session duration in minutes by the perceived exertion rating on a 0–10 scale. The result is usually expressed in arbitrary units.

Is a higher score always better?

No. A higher score simply represents a longer and/or harder session by this method. Interpret it in the context of the overall training plan and recovery.
