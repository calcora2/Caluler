Series Capacitance Calculator

Calculate equivalent capacitance for capacitors connected in series.

Enter your values to calculate.

Formula and method

1/Ctotal = 1/C1 + 1/C2 + … + 1/Cn.

How to use

  1. Use the fields labelled: Capacitances (F, comma-separated). Enter valid values or choose required options, following the units and optional-field notes shown beside each field.
  2. Select Calculate to run Series Capacitance Calculator and display the result.
  3. Check the result and units, then compare the output with the formula or method notes before applying the result.

Example

Enter capacitor values in farads; the reciprocal of equivalent capacitance is the sum of reciprocals. For example, 0.000010 F and 0.000020 F in series give Ctotal = 1 / (1/C1 + 1/C2) = about 0.00000667 F (6.67 microfarads).

Safety note

These calculators are for educational estimates. De-energize circuits before changing wiring; high voltage and stored energy can be dangerous. Verify designs against component ratings and a qualified reference.
