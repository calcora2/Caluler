About this Sleep Calculator

Choose a planned wake-up time to see suggested bedtimes based on estimates of 90-minute sleep cycles and a 15-minute allowance to fall asleep. Actual sleep needs and cycles vary from person to person.

How to use

  1. Choose the planned wake-up time.
  2. Select Calculate Bedtime.
  3. Compare the suggestions for three, four, five, and six estimated cycles.

Formula and worked example

The planner estimates bedtime by subtracting a selected number of 90-minute cycles and a 15-minute sleep-onset allowance from the chosen wake-up time. Five cycles gives 7.5 hours of assumed sleep; with the allowance, bedtime is 7 hours 45 minutes before wake-up.

Sleep cycles vary between people and through the night, so the 90-minute cycle is only a planning approximation. Use regular sleep routines and consult a healthcare professional for persistent sleep problems.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the bedtime options calculated?

The planner subtracts assumed 90-minute cycles and a 15-minute sleep-onset allowance from the selected wake-up time.

Are sleep cycles always 90 minutes?

No. Cycle length and time to fall asleep vary between people and from night to night.

Should I choose the shortest option?

No. Do not sacrifice sufficient total sleep to match a cycle estimate.
