About this Body Fat Calculator

This calculator combines the two circumference equations already represented by Caluler’s Body Fat and Army Body Fat pages. It is a tape-measure estimate, not a direct body-composition measurement and not an official military eligibility assessment.

Formula

For the male equation: body-fat percentage = 495 ÷ [1.0324 − 0.19077 × log₁₀(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log₁₀(height)] − 450.

For the female equation: body-fat percentage = 495 ÷ [1.29579 − 0.35004 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log₁₀(height)] − 450. Measurements are entered in centimetres for these coefficients.

Worked example

With the male equation, height 180 cm, waist 85 cm and neck 38 cm gives an estimate of approximately 16.1%. Small measurement differences can alter the result.

Measurement notes

Measure consistently without compressing the skin and use the same units for every circumference. For a female-equation estimate, hip circumference is required. If the measurement combination makes a logarithm invalid, the calculator will ask you to correct the inputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official Army assessment?

No. This page uses the U.S. Navy circumference equations represented in the legacy tools. Official military standards can use separate procedures and eligibility tables.

Why do I need neck and waist measurements?

The equations estimate body density from height and circumference relationships, then convert the density estimate to body-fat percentage.

Can the result be exact?

No. It is an estimate and may differ from results from other methods. Measurement technique and individual body shape affect accuracy.
