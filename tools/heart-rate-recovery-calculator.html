One-minute recovery difference

Compare the pulse at exercise end with the reading after one minute using the same measurement protocol.

Formula

One-minute heart-rate recovery = heart rate recorded at the end of exercise − heart rate recorded after one minute of recovery.

End HR − 1-minute HR → Recovery (bpm)

Worked example

If heart rate is 170 bpm at the end of exercise and 145 bpm after one minute, recovery is 170 − 145 = 25 bpm.

Interpretation

Use a consistent exercise and recovery protocol for comparisons. Results depend on exercise intensity, body position, timing, medication and health conditions. This calculator does not apply a universal pass/fail threshold or diagnose a heart condition.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is one-minute heart-rate recovery?

It is the heart rate at the end of exercise minus the heart rate measured one minute into recovery.

How should I take the readings?

Use the same timing and recovery protocol for meaningful comparisons. Note the exercise intensity, body position and any medicines that affect heart rate.

Does this calculator diagnose heart problems?

No. It reports a difference in beats per minute. It does not apply a universal pass/fail threshold or replace professional evaluation.
