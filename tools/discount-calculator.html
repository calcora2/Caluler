About the Discount & Sale Price Calculator

Compare an original price with sequential discounts, then optionally estimate tax on the discounted amount. The second discount applies after the first rather than adding percentage points to it.

How to use

  1. Enter the original price and first discount.
  2. Optionally add a second sequential discount and a sales-tax rate.
  3. Review savings, discounted price, tax and estimated final total.

Formula and method

Price after sequential discounts = P × (1 − d1) × (1 − d2). Tax = discounted price × tax rate. Final price = discounted price + tax.

Worked example

For a 120 price with a 20% discount, the discounted price is 96 before tax. An additional 10% discount applies to 96, not 120.

Assumptions and limitations

Use the same currency unit for all monetary inputs on this page. Results are estimates based on entered assumptions. They are not personalized financial, investment, credit, legal or tax advice; confirm important decisions with authoritative terms and a qualified professional where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do two discounts add together?

No. In this tool the second discount applies to the already discounted price.

Is tax applied before or after discounts?

The optional tax is applied after the discounts in this model; local retail rules can vary.

Can a discount exceed 100%?

No. Each discount input is limited to 0–100%.

Does the tool include shipping?

No. Add shipping or other charges separately if they apply.
