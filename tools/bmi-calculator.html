BMI uses weight and height

About this BMI Calculator

Enter body weight in kilograms and height in centimeters to estimate body mass index (BMI). BMI is a screening measure for adults and is not, by itself, a diagnosis or a complete health assessment.

How to use

  1. Enter body weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter height in centimeters.
  3. Select Calculate BMI and review the estimate.

Formula and worked example

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ height in metres squared. For 70 kg and 1.75 m: 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) ≈ 22.9 kg/m².

Adult BMI bands are screening ranges and may need different interpretation by age, ethnicity, pregnancy status and body composition. BMI alone is not a diagnosis and can be misleading for some people with high muscle mass.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BMI formula?

BMI equals body weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. For 70 kg and 1.75 m, BMI is about 22.9 kg/m².

Does BMI directly measure body fat?

No. It is a screening index and does not distinguish fat from muscle or show fat distribution.

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. Interpretation can differ by age, pregnancy status, body composition and clinical context.
