About the Dividend Yield Calculator

Estimate current dividend yield and annual cash income from the share count and per-share dividend you enter. An optional growth rate creates a hypothetical next-year scenario, not a forecast.

How to use

  1. Enter the current share price, number of shares and annual dividend per share.
  2. Optionally enter an assumed dividend growth rate.
  3. Review dividend yield, current share value, annual income and scenario income.

Formula and method

Dividend yield = annual dividend per share ÷ share price × 100. Annual income = dividend per share × shares owned.

Worked example

At a share price of 50 and annual dividend of 1.50 per share, the indicated yield is 3% before tax or trading costs.

Assumptions and limitations

Use the same currency unit for all monetary inputs on this page. Results are estimates based on entered assumptions. They are not personalized financial, investment, credit, legal or tax advice; confirm important decisions with authoritative terms and a qualified professional where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is dividend income guaranteed?

No. Companies can change, reduce, suspend or omit dividends.

Does yield include capital gains?

No. Dividend yield measures distributions relative to price and excludes changes in share value.

Is the income after tax?

No. Taxes, withholding and account-specific rules are not included.

What does the growth scenario mean?

It applies the rate you enter to the current dividend as a simple hypothetical, not an expected result.
