Formula and method

F = k × |q1 × q2| / r²; k ≈ 8.9875517923 × 10⁹ N·m²/C².

How to use

  1. Use the fields labelled: Charge 1 (C), Charge 2 (C), Separation (m). Enter valid values or choose required options, following the units and optional-field notes shown beside each field.
  2. Select Calculate to run Coulomb’s Law Calculator and display the result.
  3. Check the result and units, then compare the output with the formula or method notes before applying the result.

Example

Calculate the magnitude of the electrostatic force between two point charges in vacuum. For example, q1 = 0.000001 C, q2 = 0.000002 C, and r = 0.10 m give F = k*|q1*q2|/r^2 = about 1.80 N in vacuum.

Safety note

These calculators are for educational estimates. De-energize circuits before changing wiring; high voltage and stored energy can be dangerous. Verify designs against component ratings and a qualified reference.
