💡 LED Series Resistor Calculator

Estimate the series resistor needed to limit LED current. Use suitable power ratings and verify component specifications before building a circuit.

Enter your values to calculate.

Formula and method

R = (Vsupply − VLED) / ILED.

How to use

  1. Use the fields labelled: Supply voltage (V), LED forward voltage (V), LED current (mA). Enter valid values or choose required options, following the units and optional-field notes shown beside each field.
  2. Select Calculate to run LED Series Resistor Calculator and display the result.
  3. Check the result and units, then compare the output with the formula or method notes before applying the result.

Example

Estimate the series resistor needed to limit LED current. Use suitable power ratings and verify component specifications before building a circuit. For example, a 5 V supply, 2 V LED forward drop, and 20 mA current give R = (5 - 2) / 0.020 = 150 ohms. The resistor dissipates about 0.06 W, so choose a suitable power rating with margin.

Safety note

These calculators are for educational estimates. De-energize circuits before changing wiring; high voltage and stored energy can be dangerous. Verify designs against component ratings and a qualified reference.
