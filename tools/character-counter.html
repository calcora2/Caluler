About this Character Counter

Paste text to review total characters, characters without whitespace, and word count. The text is trimmed at the beginning and end before these values are calculated.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text.
  2. Select Count Characters.
  3. Review the character totals and word count.

Calculation method & worked example

Total characters include spaces and line breaks inside the trimmed text. Characters without whitespace removes all whitespace characters. Example: Caluler tools contains 13 characters in total and 12 without whitespace.
