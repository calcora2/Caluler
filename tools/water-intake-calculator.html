About the Water Intake Calculator

This calculator applies a simplified 35 mL-per-kg estimate as a starting point, not a universal daily water requirement. Total water can come from drinks and food, and individual needs can vary with activity, climate and health circumstances.

How to use

  1. Enter body weight in kilograms and calculate the estimate.
  2. Review the result in litres and any converted units shown by the calculator; the method uses 35 mL per kilogram.
  3. Treat the number as a rough guide rather than a precise prescription. Activity, hot weather, food and individual circumstances can change fluid needs.

Formula and worked example

The tool uses a simplified estimate of 35 mL per kg of body weight: estimated litres/day = weight (kg) × 0.035. For 70 kg, that gives 2.45 L/day, approximately 83 US fluid ounces.

This is not a universal hydration prescription. Fluid needs vary with activity, heat, diet, pregnancy, medicines and medical conditions; some people need clinician-guided fluid limits.

Who can use this calculator?

This free online calculator is useful for students, professionals, businesses, creators and everyday users who need a quick answer. Results depend on the information entered and the assumptions described above.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formula does this estimate use?

It multiplies body weight in kilograms by 0.035 to estimate litres per day. For example, 70 kg gives 2.45 L.

Is this a hydration prescription?

No. Exercise, heat, diet, illness, medicines and individual circumstances can change fluid needs.

Does this track what I drink?

No. It estimates a starting amount from weight and does not track drinks or hydration status.
