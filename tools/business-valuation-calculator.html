About the Business Valuation Calculator

Estimate an enterprise value from a selected operating metric and a multiple you supply, then adjust for debt and cash to estimate equity value. Multiples depend on business quality, industry, growth, risk and transaction context.

How to use

  1. Select EBITDA or revenue multiple.
  2. Enter revenue, EBITDA, the chosen multiple, debt and cash.
  3. Review the implied enterprise value and illustrative equity value.

Formula and method

Enterprise value = selected metric × selected multiple. Illustrative equity value = enterprise value − debt + cash.

Worked example

If EBITDA is 90,000 and the chosen multiple is 4×, implied enterprise value is 360,000 before net-debt adjustments.

Assumptions and limitations

Use the same currency unit for all monetary inputs on this page. Results are estimates based on entered assumptions. They are not personalized financial, investment, credit, legal or tax advice; confirm important decisions with authoritative terms and a qualified professional where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a professional valuation?

No. It is a simple multiple-based illustration and does not replace a valuation by a qualified adviser.

Which multiple should I use?

Use a comparable and defensible multiple for the industry, size, growth and risk profile; the tool does not choose one for you.

Why subtract debt and add cash?

A common enterprise-to-equity bridge adjusts enterprise value for net debt, but deal terms can vary.

Does it include working capital?

No. Working capital, minority interests and other transaction adjustments are not modeled.
