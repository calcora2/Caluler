About the Ideal Weight Calculator

The Devine-style equation gives a height-based reference weight using a sex-based constant. It does not account for body composition, medical history or individual circumstances, so it should not be treated as a personalized health goal.

How to use

  1. Enter adult height in centimetres and select the equation option shown by the calculator.
  2. Calculate the Devine-style reference weight and review the units displayed.
  3. Treat the result as a formula-based reference only. It does not assess body composition or establish an individualized target weight.

Formula and worked example

This page uses a Devine-style reference equation with a sex-based constant: 50 kg for the male equation or 45.5 kg for the female equation, plus 2.3 kg for each inch above 5 feet. The tool floors the height adjustment at zero below 5 feet rather than extrapolating the equation downward.

At 170 cm (about 66.93 inches), the male equation gives about 65.9 kg and the female equation about 61.4 kg. These are formula outputs, not an individualized healthy-weight target.

Who can use this calculator?

This free online calculator is useful for students, professionals, businesses, creators and everyday users who need a quick answer. Results depend on the information entered and the assumptions described above.
