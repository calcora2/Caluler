About this Percentage Calculator

Enter a part and a total to find what percentage the part represents of the total. The total must not be zero because division by zero is undefined.

How to use

  1. Enter the part in the value field.
  2. Enter the whole amount in the total field.
  3. Select Calculate Percentage and review the result.

Calculation method & worked example

Percentage = part / total x 100. Example: 25 out of 200 is (25 / 200) x 100 = 12.50%. The calculation cannot use zero as the total.
