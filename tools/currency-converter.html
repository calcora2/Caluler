About the Currency Converter

Convert currencies by supplying a current exchange rate from a bank or trusted source. This calculator intentionally does not fetch market data; the output is only as current as the rate you enter.

How to use

  1. Select source and target currency labels.
  2. Enter the amount and the exchange rate expressed as target currency units per one source unit.
  3. Add any conversion fee percentage and review the estimated converted amount.

Formula and method

Fee amount = source amount × fee rate. Amount after fee = source amount − fee. Converted amount = amount after fee × exchange rate.

Worked example

If 100 source units convert at 0.79 target units per source unit with no fee, the estimated converted amount is 79 target units.

Assumptions and limitations

Enter a manually verified rate; this tool does not retrieve live quotes. Results are estimates based on entered assumptions. They are not personalized financial, investment, credit, legal or tax advice; confirm important decisions with authoritative terms and a qualified professional where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this fetch live exchange rates?

No. Enter a rate from a source you trust and check when it was quoted.

How should I enter the rate?

Enter target currency units for one unit of the source currency.

Are bank fees included?

Only the fee percentage you enter is included; fixed fees, spreads and taxes may apply separately.

Can this be used for accounting?

Use a verified rate, date and accounting policy for formal records; this is a quick conversion estimate.
