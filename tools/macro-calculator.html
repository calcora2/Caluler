About the Macro & Protein Calculator

This calculator combines two related nutrition calculations: a calorie-based protein/carbohydrate/fat split and a body-weight-based daily protein estimate.

How to use

  1. Select Calorie-based macro split to enter daily calories and protein, carbohydrate and fat percentages. The percentages must total 100%.
  2. Select Protein target by body weight to enter weight and a grams-per-kilogram factor.
  3. Select Calculate and review the gram estimates and units.

Formulas and worked examples

For a calorie-based split: protein grams = calories × protein percentage ÷ 100 ÷ 4; carbohydrate grams use 4 kcal/g; fat grams use 9 kcal/g.

Example: 2,000 kcal with 30% protein, 40% carbohydrates and 30% fat gives 150 g protein, 200 g carbohydrates and about 66.7 g fat.

For the protein-target mode: daily protein estimate = body weight (kg) × selected factor (g/kg). Example: 70 kg × 1.6 g/kg = 112 g/day.

Important note

These are mathematical estimates. A protein factor or macro split is not automatically appropriate for every person; needs vary with age, activity, health and clinical context.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the two modes?

The macro-split mode converts a calorie target and percentages into protein, carbohydrate and fat grams. The protein mode estimates daily protein grams from body weight and a selected factor.

Must the macro percentages total 100%?

Yes. In calorie-based macro mode, the protein, carbohydrate and fat percentages must sum to 100%.

How is a protein target calculated?

Multiply body weight in kilograms by the selected protein factor in grams per kilogram. The factor is user-selected and should not be treated as an individualized prescription.

Are the results a personalized diet recommendation?

No. The calculator performs arithmetic based on your entries. Discuss personalized nutrition needs with a qualified professional when appropriate.
