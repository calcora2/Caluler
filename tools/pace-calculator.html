Distance and time determine running pace

Pace is elapsed time divided by distance. This calculator accepts kilometres and minutes and reports average speed in km/h.

About the Pace Calculator

Running pace is elapsed time per kilometre, while average speed is distance covered per hour. This calculator reports averages from the distance and time entered; hills, stops, changing effort and GPS accuracy are not represented.

How to use

  1. Enter the distance in kilometres and the total elapsed time in minutes.
  2. Calculate the result to view average pace in minutes per kilometre and average speed in kilometres per hour.
  3. Use the same distance and timing method when comparing runs. The output is an average and does not describe pace changes during the route.

Formula and worked example

Pace = elapsed time ÷ distance. Speed in km/h = distance (km) ÷ elapsed time (hours). A 5 km run in 30 minutes gives 6:00 min/km and an average speed of 10 km/h.

Use the total elapsed time, including any breaks that you want included in the pace. GPS distance and rounding can affect the displayed result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is running pace calculated?

Pace in minutes per kilometre equals elapsed time in minutes divided by distance in kilometres. Speed is distance divided by elapsed time in hours.

Which units does this page use?

Enter kilometres and elapsed minutes; the page reports min/km and km/h.

Why might a watch differ?

GPS measurement, rounding, pauses, moving time and elapsed time can produce differences.
