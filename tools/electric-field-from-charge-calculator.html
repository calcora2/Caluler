🌀 Electric Field from Charge Calculator

Calculate the magnitude of the electric field produced by a point charge at a specified distance in vacuum.

Enter your values to calculate.

Formula and method

E = k × |q| / r².

How to use

  1. Use the fields labelled: Charge (C), Distance (m). Enter valid values or choose required options, following the units and optional-field notes shown beside each field.
  2. Select Calculate to run Electric Field from Charge Calculator and display the result.
  3. Check the result and units, then compare the output with the formula or method notes before applying the result.

Example

Calculate the magnitude of the electric field produced by a point charge at a specified distance in vacuum. For example, q = +0.000001 C at r = 0.50 m gives E = k*|q|/r^2 = about 35,950 N/C in vacuum.

Safety note

These calculators are for educational estimates. De-energize circuits before changing wiring; high voltage and stored energy can be dangerous. Verify designs against component ratings and a qualified reference.
