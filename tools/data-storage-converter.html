About the Data Storage Converter

Convert bits, bytes, decimal storage units from KB to QB, and binary units from KiB to QiB. This Caluler tool is designed for quick, transparent calculations with clearly labeled inputs.

How to use

  1. Enter the values requested above.
  2. Select the source and target units. Choose decimal units for powers of 1,000 or binary units for powers of 1,024.

Formula & assumptions

Supports bits, bytes, SI decimal prefixes (1 KB = 1,000 bytes), and IEC binary prefixes (1 KiB = 1,024 bytes). A byte is treated as 8 bits. Ronna/quetta and robi/quebi are included as extended large prefixes.
