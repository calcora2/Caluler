Fat-Free Mass Index Calculator

Calculate fat-free mass and fat-free mass index from body weight, height and a body-fat percentage estimate.

🦾 Calculator

Enter weight, height and a body-fat estimate.

Fat-free mass adjusted for height

FFMI divides estimated fat-free mass in kilograms by height in metres squared.

Formula

Fat-free mass = weight × (1 − body-fat percentage ÷ 100). FFMI = fat-free mass ÷ height in metres squared.

Weight + body fat → Fat-free mass ÷ Height² → FFMI

Worked example

For 75 kg, 180 cm and an estimated 15% body fat, fat-free mass = 75 × 0.85 = 63.75 kg. FFMI = 63.75 ÷ 1.8² ≈ 19.68 kg/m².

Limitations

FFMI depends on the quality of the body-fat estimate and does not directly measure muscle mass. There is no single interpretation range that applies to every sex, age or population; this calculator does not diagnose health or athletic status.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is FFMI calculated?

Calculate fat-free mass from weight and body-fat percentage, then divide fat-free mass in kilograms by height in metres squared.

Does FFMI directly measure muscle?

No. It uses estimated fat-free mass and is influenced by the accuracy of the body-fat input.

Is there one universal FFMI target?

No universal category applies to every person. This calculator gives a mathematical index and does not diagnose health or athletic status.
