About the Final Grade Calculator

Calculate the final grade needed on an exam based on current grade and exam weight. This Caluler tool is designed for quick, transparent calculations with clearly labeled inputs.

How to use

  1. Use the fields labelled: Current grade %, Final exam weight %, Target overall grade %. Enter valid values or choose required options, following the units and optional-field notes shown beside each field.
  2. Select Calculate to run Final Grade Calculator and display the result.
  3. Check the result and units, then compare the output with the formula or method notes before applying the result.

Formula & assumptions

Required exam = (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight.

Who can use this calculator?

This free online calculator is useful for students, professionals, businesses, creators and everyday users who need a quick answer. Results depend on the information entered and the assumptions described above.
