Health-related results, including body measurements, calorie estimates, nutrition figures, heart-rate values and other wellness calculations, are general informational outputs. They do not diagnose a condition, assess your complete medical history, prescribe treatment or replace advice from a doctor or other qualified healthcare professional.

Individual needs can vary with age, health history, medication, pregnancy, activity, measurement method and other factors that a calculator may not consider. Do not use a result to start, stop or change treatment or medication. Seek professional care for personal medical concerns.

5. Fitness, Exercise and Nutrition Estimates

Fitness, calorie, pace, training and nutrition tools use estimates or simplified models. Actual energy use, performance, hydration, body composition and nutritional needs can differ from calculated values. Device measurements and manual inputs may also be inaccurate.

Use these outputs as general planning information, not as a substitute for individual coaching or clinical nutrition advice. Stop an activity and seek appropriate help if you experience concerning symptoms or feel unsafe.

Financial calculators may estimate payments, interest, savings, returns, budgets, taxes, fees, cash flow or other figures using the inputs and assumptions selected by the visitor. Investment values can rise or fall, and past performance does not guarantee future results. Review the relevant provider documents and obtain independent financial advice when appropriate.

7. Loans, Interest and Repayment Estimates

Loan and repayment calculations are illustrative unless the calculator specifically states that it uses a verified offer for your circumstances. Actual repayment amounts and total costs may differ because of fees, variable rates, payment dates, insurance, penalties, taxes, rounding, lender policies or changes to the agreement.

Always check the lender's official repayment schedule, key facts, disclosures and signed agreement before making a borrowing decision.

8. Tax and Accounting Calculators

Tax, payroll, salary and accounting outputs may use simplified rates, thresholds, deductions or assumptions. Tax rules and reporting requirements can change and may differ between countries, states, provinces, territories and tax years. Personal circumstances, residency, filing status, income type and available reliefs can materially affect the result.

Do not use a calculator result as the sole basis for structural design, electrical work, construction, machinery operation or another safety-critical task. Have specifications and calculations checked by a qualified professional and follow applicable standards, codes and manufacturer instructions.

You are responsible for entering complete and accurate information. Before using a result, check every number, decimal separator, date, time zone, selected option and unit. A value entered in the wrong unit or format can produce a result that appears plausible but is incorrect.

Small differences can also arise from decimal representation, display settings or different rounding conventions.

For payments, technical specifications, official forms or other exact-use cases, check the required precision and independently verify the unrounded values where available.
