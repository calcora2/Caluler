Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information handled when you use Caluler tools, browser settings and contact features.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Privacy at a Glance

Caluler is a collection of free online calculators and practical browser-based tools. Most tools can be used without creating an account. We aim to request only the information needed to operate the website, respond to messages, remember basic preferences and protect the service.

  • You can use most calculators without registering or providing your name or email address.
  • Values entered into ordinary calculator fields are generally processed by the calculator code in your browser to produce a result. The contact form is a separate service and sends the message fields to FormSubmit for delivery.
  • The site stores theme and language preferences in your browser and uses a Google Translate language cookie when you choose another language.
  • Third-party providers may process technical information when they host, translate, protect or deliver parts of the website.
  • Where advertising is enabled, advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies as described below.

Please do not enter passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, medical records or other confidential information into a calculator or contact form.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy applies to Caluler at https://caluler.work/, including its calculator pages, tools, navigation, language controls and contact page. It explains the information that may be handled when you browse the website or choose to send us a message.

In this policy, “Caluler”, “we”, “our” and “us” refer to the people responsible for operating the Caluler website. “You” and “visitor” refer to a person accessing the website. Third-party providers such as hosting, translation and form-processing services operate under their own terms and privacy notices.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Information you choose to provide

You are not asked to create an account to use most Caluler tools. If you contact us, the contact form may ask for your name, email address, subject and message. We use those details to receive, understand and respond to your enquiry, report or suggestion.

Technical information

When a browser requests a website page, the hosting and delivery infrastructure may process ordinary technical information such as an IP address, the requested page, date and time of the request, browser and device characteristics, referring page, response status and security or error information. This information may be handled by the hosting provider to deliver pages, maintain reliability, detect abuse and troubleshoot problems. The provider's own privacy statement explains its processing.

Caluler is published from a GitHub repository and may be served through GitHub Pages. When GitHub provides the hosting and delivery infrastructure, GitHub may process visitor and website-usage information such as IP address, device and browser details, request time, referring page and pages viewed. This processing is governed by GitHub's General Privacy Statement.

Browser preferences

Caluler saves your selected Light or Dark theme and selected language in browser storage so the settings can be restored as you move between pages. These preference values are not an account profile and are not used by Caluler to identify you by name.

3. Calculator Inputs and Results

Caluler's ordinary calculators are designed to use the values you enter to perform a calculation and display a result in the page. In the website source reviewed for this policy, ordinary calculator inputs are handled by the calculator page scripts; the tools do not require you to sign in or submit those values to a Caluler account.

This description is about the ordinary calculator experience and is not a promise that every browser request, third-party resource or future tool operates without network processing. Your browser must request the page and its scripts from the hosting infrastructure, and some features such as translation or the contact form use external providers. A tool-specific notice will apply if a future feature sends information to an external service.

Calculator outputs are displayed to help you complete a task. They should not be treated as a secure record, a saved account history or a substitute for independently checking important results. Avoid entering information that would identify another person or expose confidential financial, health, business or personal details.

4. Contact Form and Email Messages

If you submit the Contact Caluler form, the information in its name, email, subject and message fields is sent through FormSubmit using its AJAX form endpoint. FormSubmit processes the submission, applies its form and spam-handling features, and forwards the message to Caluler.work@gmail.com.

FormSubmit's published documentation states that form submissions are retained for 30 days. The provider may use automated spam-prevention controls, which can include Google reCAPTCHA and a hidden honeypot field. A visible checkbox is not necessarily shown for every visitor. FormSubmit controls its own service, processing and retention; see its help information and documentation for current details.

After delivery, the message may also remain in the Caluler email inbox until it is deleted under the mailbox provider's controls and retention practices. If you email us directly, your email provider and our mailbox provider will process that communication as part of delivering and storing the email.

Only send information needed for your enquiry. Do not submit passwords, one-time codes, payment details, identity documents, confidential client information or sensitive health information. If you include your email address, we may use it to reply to the message you sent.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

Caluler uses a small amount of browser storage to remember basic settings:

  • Theme preference: the browser's local storage key caluler-theme records the selected Light or Dark appearance.
  • Language preference: the local storage key caluler-language remembers the selected language.
  • Translation cookie: when you select a language other than English, the Google Translate integration may set a googtrans cookie so the language choice can be restored. The current site script sets this preference for up to one year.
  • Session storage: a temporary session value may be used to prevent repeated page reloads while the language control initializes. Session storage is generally cleared when the browser tab or session ends.

These technologies help the site remember your preferences. You can clear local storage, cookies or session storage through your browser settings. If you block them, the website should remain viewable, but theme or language preferences may not persist or some functions may behave differently.

6. Google Translate and External Website Resources

Caluler loads Google's website translation script so visitors can choose from the languages offered by the site. If you use the language control, page content and technical request information may be processed by Google to provide the translation experience. Google's services are governed by Google's own privacy practices.

Read the Google Privacy Policy and Google information about cookies for details about Google's processing. Caluler does not control Google's independent services, cookies, availability or retention practices.

7. Advertising and Personalized Ads

Caluler may display advertising when advertising features are enabled on the website. If Google advertising or another advertising network is used, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, limit the frequency of or personalize advertisements. Advertising partners may use information about visits to this website or other websites, subject to their own policies and your available privacy choices.

Google's publisher guidance explains that Google advertising cookies may help Google and its partners show ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this or other websites. You can review or change Google's available advertising choices at Google Ads Settings. You can also review broader industry opt-out information at YourAdChoices.

Advertising availability and the technologies used may change. Your browser, device, location and applicable law can affect which cookies or advertising choices are available. Blocking advertising cookies may reduce personalization but does not necessarily remove all advertising.

8. How Information Is Used

Information described in this policy may be used, as appropriate, to:

  • provide the pages, calculators, converters and requested website features;
  • remember your theme and language preferences;
  • receive, route and respond to contact messages, feedback and support requests;
  • protect the website and its visitors, detect spam or abuse, and investigate technical faults;
  • maintain, troubleshoot and improve the reliability, accessibility and presentation of the website;
  • serve or measure advertising where advertising is enabled and permitted; and
  • meet lawful requests or protect legal rights, safety and security where necessary.

9. When Information May Be Shared

Caluler does not sell personal information as a standalone business. Information may be made available to service providers only where reasonably needed for the activities described in this policy, including:

  • Website hosting and delivery providers that make Caluler pages available and help maintain security and performance;
  • FormSubmit to receive, filter and deliver contact-form submissions;
  • Google when you use the translation feature, and when Google advertising services are enabled;
  • Email providers that deliver and store messages sent to or from Caluler; and
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law, necessary to respond to a valid legal request, or reasonably needed to protect rights, safety or the website against abuse.

Third-party providers may process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Their practices are not controlled by Caluler.

10. How Long Information Is Kept

Caluler aims to keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was handled, subject to legal, security and operational needs. FormSubmit's current published documentation states a 30-day retention period for contact-form submissions on its service. Copies delivered to the Caluler email inbox may remain there until deleted. Browser preferences remain in your browser until they expire or you clear them; the translation cookie described above may last up to one year. Hosting-provider logs and third-party service records are retained according to the relevant provider's own practices.

11. International Processing

Caluler visitors may access the website from different countries. Hosting, translation, form-processing, advertising and email providers may operate infrastructure or support teams in countries other than the visitor's country. As a result, information handled by those providers may be processed internationally under their terms and the safeguards required by applicable law.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable practical measures to maintain the website and limit unnecessary access to information we receive. However, no website, email service, internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use care when deciding what to submit online and avoid sending confidential information through the contact form.

13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, receive a portable copy, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or make a complaint to a privacy regulator. These rights are subject to local law, exceptions and the identity checks needed to protect information.

You can also choose not to send a contact form, clear browser storage or cookies, change your language or theme preference, and use available advertising controls. If you want to ask about a contact message you submitted, contact us from the email address used in that message where possible and explain what you want us to do. We may need to verify the request before taking action.

To make a privacy request or ask a question, email Caluler.work@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data-protection or privacy authority where applicable.

14. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent personal information through the contact form, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.
